Festora Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Festora is a local iOS app for holiday decoration checklists, area tasks, closeout counts, and reuse templates. This Privacy Policy explains how Festora handles information on your device.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Festora mobile application distributed on the App Store. You do not need a Festora cloud account to use the core checklist features.

Information stored on your device

  • Season titles, area labels, task notes, planned and placed counts, and closeout values you enter.
  • Photo copies you attach as decoration or closeout references.
  • Optional dictated note text produced when you use speech-to-text.
  • Local preference flags such as onboarding completion and gift credit balances.
  • Verified in-app purchase transaction identifiers needed for local entitlement bookkeeping.

Festora does not upload your season photos or notes to a Festora business server as part of the core checklist workflow.

Permissions

Festora requests system permissions only when related actions are triggered, except for the first-home prompts described below:

  • App Tracking Transparency — a system prompt may appear the first time you reach the home Records tab. Festora does not use tracking identifiers for advertising measurement in this package after the prompt.
  • Notifications — a system prompt may appear after the tracking prompt on first home entry. Festora does not schedule or deliver product notifications after the prompt.
  • Camera — when you tap Take Photo on a task.
  • Photo Library — when you choose reference photos or save an image to Photos.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition — when you start dictation for a task note.

If you deny a permission, features that depend on it stay unavailable while the rest of the app continues to work.

Purchases

Optional consumable gift packs are processed by Apple In-App Purchase. Apple handles payment credentials. Festora stores purchase results locally so pack credits can be applied on this device.

Analytics and tracking

Festora may show the App Tracking Transparency system dialog once. This package does not consume IDFA for advertising workflows after that dialog. Festora does not sell personal information.

Data retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete a season or task media, clear local data in Settings, or remove the app. Clearing local data removes seasons, purchase history stored by the app, and preference flags from this installation. Export or share actions you start yourself may create copies outside the app that Festora cannot revoke.

Children

Festora is intended for a general audience that can manage personal photos and home decoration records. Do not submit sensitive information about children through photos or notes.

Contact

For privacy questions about Festora, use the support channel listed on the App Store product page for Festora.
